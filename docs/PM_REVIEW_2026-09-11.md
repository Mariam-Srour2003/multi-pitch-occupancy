## The one-paragraph version

Day two named the defect class: **guards that existed and did not operate**. Day three found
that the class extends past guards to *results* — three published numbers were artefacts of
the axis they were measured on, one published table was built from a superseded input, one
experiment could not run at all, and one headline did not survive being computed a second
time with a different random seed. The system also turned out to have no classifier: the
`classify` seam had never been given one outside a test, so the whole pipeline had been
exercised end to end without a frame ever being classified by it. Two of the project's own
recommendations were reversed on measurement — the C3 fallback and the input ablation's best
variant. Nothing here was found by a test failing; everything was found by asking whether a
number could be reproduced, and most of the checks that found them are now tests.

## The pattern worth naming: existence is not health

The pipeline's `--check` asks whether an output **exists**. Three separate failures hid behind
that, at three different depths.

| | What existed | What was not true |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `figs/accuracy_vs_latency.png`, `project_site.html` | Built from latency numbers that had been re-measured that morning. Nothing compared an output against its inputs. |
| 2 | `false_play_rescored.csv` | Covered **52 of the search's 88** evaluations — the rescoring ran thirteen hours before the search JSON was finalised. Re-run: 88 rows, **none of the 52 moved**. |
| 3 | `input_ablation.csv` | Its script **could not run**: `vars()` on a slotted `PreprocessConfig` raises. Second time this script has been un-runnable while its numbers were cited. |

`reproduce_all --check` now reports staleness from **git** rather than modification times.
That distinction was itself a finding: the mtime version reported four stale stages on this
repository and **all four were false positives** — a rerun had rewritten a file byte-for-byte.
A check that cries wolf on first contact is worse than no check, because the next person
learns to skim past it.

## Results that were artefacts of their axis

The project established twice that cross-venue **play recall is free**: every held-out venue
is 100% active play, so a constant predictor scores 1.000 and a model that says PLAY more
often is rewarded for it. Two more tables were being read on that axis without the control.

**The input ablation's best variant never identifies an empty pitch.**

| variant | recall | false-play | empty accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| `full` | 0.960 | 0.230 | 0.770 |
| **`grayscale`** | **0.982** | **0.021** | **0.979** |
| `blur4` | 0.929 | 1.000 | 0.000 |
| `crop50` | **0.998** | 0.313 | **0.000** |
| `gray+crop50` | 0.899 | 0.988 | 0.012 |

`crop50` led that table and has empty accuracy **0.000**. The cited reading — *removal has a
floor: grayscale helped, the crop helped more, both together fell below baseline* — was
two-thirds artefact. Grayscale is the real finding and was previously indistinguishable from
it: the only removal that improves **both** axes. Corrected in six places including the drawn
diagram, which now carries the false-play rate in each box.

**`class_balancing.csv` computed its control and did not write it down.** The recommendation
rests entirely on it — unweighted looks +0.0286 *better* on recall while calling 46.5% of
held-out empty pitches a match against balanced's 23.1% — and a reader who opened the artefact
rather than the log would have drawn the opposite conclusion from the same run.

## A headline that did not survive a second seed

`light` augmentation was reported at **0.8550** macro-F1 with empty-pitch recall 0.687. Its
own write-up noted that only one draw had been taken. That note was the finding.

| draw | 99 | 7 | 123 | 13 | **42** *(published)* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| macro-F1 | 0.3479 | 0.3501 | 0.3510 | 0.4136 | **0.8550** |

Median 0.3510, sd 0.2206, and **four of five fall below the 0.4406 the probe reaches with no
augmentation at all**. The published number is the maximum of five. Retracted across seven
surfaces.

The same check was then run on STAN, the other result that consumed a draw once, and came back
**differently**: the ordering survives (STAN is first in five draws of five) while the ceiling
does not (1.0000 in three of five). That corrected an overstatement — *"the benchmark is
saturated"* describes a composition, not the design — and it is why the three unbuilt modules
are now marked **blocked on measurement rather than effort**: a benchmark that hits its
ceiling in most draws cannot rank a new architecture against the current one.

## The pipeline had no head on it

`run_slot` and `run_due` have taken a `classify` callable since they were written. **Nothing
outside a test ever supplied one.** Every scheduler test hands the seam a stub,
`end_to_end_slots.py` rebuilds each slot from the *label* column, and `worker.main` raised
`NotImplementedError` naming exactly this. Sampling, fusion, aggregation, evidence selection
and reconciliation had all been exercised end to end without a single frame ever having been
classified by the system.

`vision/classifier.py` fills it, its input path reproduces the cached features exactly, and it
trains on development rows only so the venue lock holds in deployment. Running it found three
defects — the best being that `run_due` persists only when handed a connection, so the first
version classified both slots, wrote nothing, and printed *"2 slot(s) written to the
database"*.

## Two recommendations reversed on measurement

**WP2-T11 (the C3 fallback)** was recommended as Option C — drop to two classes. Measured, that
takes DINOv2 from calling 31% of unfamiliar empty pitches "play" to calling **all** of them
that. The six-frame class is absorbing 69% of what the model cannot place, and because C3 maps
to NOTUSED/REVIEW while ACTIVE_PLAY maps to USED, it converts would-be **billing errors** into
correct-or-reviewable verdicts. The class stays; the *claim* shrinks.

**H4's speed clause** failed on one reading and now fails on both. The committed latency table
had been measured under load — as its own docstring warned, and it was never re-run. Idle,
ConvNeXtV2 reads 100.5 ms against 150.9. Contention costs the heavier model more, so load
**inflates** a ratio between models of different weight: the bias pointed at the compact
backbone, and was enough to carry the single-frame line over a 2× bar it does not clear.

## Things that could have accused someone

Two, both fixed, both worth stating plainly because reconciliation implicates named staff.

**A stale booking export.** Reconciliation does not lose the comparison when the export stops
short — it makes it anyway and is confidently wrong. Every slot past the last day matches no
booking, and an unbooked slot showing play is **SERIOUS**. A month-old export would hand an
operator a page of serious anomalies against a facility that did nothing, each of which
`authority.py` requires a human to confirm. A day outside the export's span now returns
NEEDS_REVIEW, checked before anything serious can fire.

**An unredacted figure.** `augmentation_grid.jpg` is committed *and served*, and its first tile
was a night match with six unpixelated players — while `ethics.md` committed to blurring faces
in any published figure and `explain.py` called its redaction "not optional and not a flag".
It now redacts, reports the box count rather than the word, and refuses to write the sheet if
the detector will not load.

not be fixed by picking a number — the mistake this project has now met four times.
