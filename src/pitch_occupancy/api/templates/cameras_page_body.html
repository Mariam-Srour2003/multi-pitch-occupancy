What is happening now?

Takes a picture from each camera of one pitch right now and says whether the pitch is empty, being played on, or used for something else. People seen by both cameras are added together; a ball seen by either camera counts.

This is a spot check: nothing here is recorded. The history on the dashboard comes only from the live schedule below. A check takes a few seconds per camera.

Reading the cameras...

Start recording a day

Tell the system when the pitch's slots run. Every slot is watched one picture per camera per minute, and the result (empty / playing / other use) is saved at the end of the slot.

Saving replaces this pitch's existing slots and keeps every other pitch's. The previous schedule is kept as a backup.
